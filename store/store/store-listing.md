## Full description (max 4000 characters)

One orb. One finger. Every ~16 seconds, reality glitches and the game **morphs into a different genre** — mid-play, no loading screen, no warning. You keep your position, momentum, score, and hearts through every mutation. The game has opinions about all of this and shares them constantly.

**28 GENRES**, and counting your patience:
Runner, cave-flier, twin-stick shooter, breakout (you ARE the ball), snake, rhythm, traffic-hopping, tower building, bullet-graze dodging, memory patterns, tile-flipping, orbit-swinging, mini-golf, whack-a-mole, fruit-slicing, mirror-match, fishing, button-mashing sumo, asteroid dodging, pachinko, base defense, wall-climbing, frog-crossing, snake-eating, laser-reflecting, rhythm-parrying, a Pac-Man-style maze chase, a Spider-Man-style grapple swing — and sometimes, with no warning at all, a cat appears and the only objective is to pet it. Mind the tail.

**3 ROTATING BOSSES** after every full cycle: VS PONG (first to 3 — it can miss now), THE CURSOR (dodge its clicks, tap it while it's not looking), and the WORM KING (chase the golden tail, avoid everything else).

**7 WORLDS** reskin the whole game each cycle: Neon City, Deep Space (low gravity), The Deep (underwater), Lava Core (faster), Retro Arcade (CRT scanlines), Glitch Void (mutators doubled), and Candy Core (sticky, pastel, suspicious).

**MUTATORS** start rolling from cycle 2: TINY BIT, GIANT BIT, TURBO, LIGHTS OUT (spotlight only), GOLD RUSH (2× score), SLOW MO.

**Surprises along the way:** coin rain, mystery gifts, a golden ghost worth chasing, a rare TIME WARP, score-milestone fireworks.

**COMPETE:**
• HALL OF WHATEVER — the in-game leaderboard, with arcade-style initials entry and crowns for the top 3.
• VS — pass-and-play for two, same seeded universe for both runs, so it's pure skill, not luck.
• DAILY CHALLENGE — one shared seed per day. Everyone gets the exact same run, so bragging about your score is an actual fair comparison.
• 12 BADGES to chase, tracked live on the title screen.
• Share your score with one tap.

Everything is procedural — every sound effect, every backing track, every pixel of art — generated on the fly. No ads. No trackers. No network requests. No in-app purchases. It just runs.

Controls: one finger does everything, portrait or landscape. Tap the on-screen ✕ MENU button or hit Esc any time to bail out mid-run.

*The cat forgives you in advance.*
