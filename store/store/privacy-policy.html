Privacy Policy

The Whatever Game

This game collects no personal data, uses no analytics, and makes no network requests. Everything runs entirely on your device.

What data the app collects

None. The Whatever Game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics of any kind. The app makes no network requests while you play — it does not talk to any server, ours or anyone else's.

What is stored, and where

To remember your best score, your local leaderboard entries, your chosen initials, and which badges you've earned between sessions, the app saves a small amount of data using your device's standard local storage (the same mechanism any offline app or website uses to remember settings). This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is not visible to us, to Google, or to any third party
  • is only ever read by the app itself, to show it back to you
  • can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's data/storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app

Third-party services

The app uses none. No ad networks, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, no social logins, no in-app purchase processors. There is nothing running in the background and nothing phoning home.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either. The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no way for players to communicate with each other or with us.

Permissions

The app requests no special device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, files, or any other sensitive data.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if a future update adds an optional online leaderboard — this page will be updated first, and the change will describe exactly what is collected and why, before it ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer at the contact address listed on the app's Google Play store page.
